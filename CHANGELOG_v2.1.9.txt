SaucedCarts v2.1.9
Released: 2026-05-29
====================

A world-spawning rework. Parking-lot carts at retail stores, smarter
performance, and probabilities that actually do what they say.


WHAT'S NEW
----------

* Carts now occasionally spawn in the PARKING LOT of grocery stores,
  gigamarts, department stores, warehouses, and grocery-storage areas.
  An abandoned cart in front of an abandoned shop -- the way it would
  look in real life. Spawn rate is unchanged; only WHERE a cart can
  land. Most multi-cart shops will end up with a mix of indoor and
  parking-lot carts.

* New sandbox option: "Enable Parking-Lot Spawns" (default ON). Turn it
  off if you'd rather keep all carts strictly inside.


CHANGED: WORLD-SPAWN PROBABILITIES
----------------------------------

Spawn rates now work the way the labels imply.

  * "Spawn chance" is the chance the building spawns ANY carts today --
    one roll per building, not per square or per cart attempt.
  * "Max Carts Per Building" is the cap on how many carts spawn in a
    building that does spawn (uniform 1 to cap).

Previously the chance was rolled once per cart-attempt, so a 12% garage
chance at cap=5 actually produced carts in nearly half of garages. Now
12% means 12%. If you've cranked up "Max Carts Per Building" expect
more carts per shop, not a higher rate of shops having carts.

Storage / garage chances were lowered alongside this change so the
overall distribution stays similar to v2.1.8 at the default cap=1:

  storageunit   15 -> 8
  garagestorage 12 -> 5
  storage        8 -> 4
  shed           2 -> 1

If you preferred the v2.1.8 density at cap > 1, raise the storage
chances back up via an addon override or wait for a future option to
tune individual room rates from the sandbox.


PERFORMANCE
-----------

World spawning -- rewrote the hook to mirror how vanilla spawns
vehicles:

  * Hooks LoadChunk (once per chunk) instead of LoadGridsquare (once
    per square).
  * Builds a one-time index of cart-eligible buildings at world load.
    Chunks with nothing to spawn return instantly.
  * Outdoor placement uses PZ's VehicleZone system directly -- no
    tile-by-tile parking-lot scan during play.

Most LoadChunk events now finish in a single hash lookup. The savings
matter most on dedicated servers, where chunks stream constantly as
players move.

Inventory transfers -- the cart's transfer hook now bails to the
vanilla path immediately when neither side of a transfer involves a
cart, instead of classifying every transfer in the world. The vast
majority of transfers (chest-to-chest, looting, crafting) never touch
a cart, so they no longer pay any SaucedCarts overhead.

Server-to-client traffic -- the world-spawn tracker no longer
broadcasts its (ever-growing) building-decision table to clients on
every chunk; it was server-only bookkeeping that no client read. One
less packet stream on dedicated servers as players explore.


COMPATIBILITY
-------------

Save-safe. No save-data changes -- safe to upgrade mid-save. Buildings
that already spawned a cart on v2.1.8 stay decided; only newly streamed
buildings use the new spawn logic.

Addon authors: the public spawn-registration API is unchanged.
`allowOutdoor = true` opts an entry into outdoor placement; an optional
`outdoorWeight` (0-100, default 30) controls how often that entry's
carts land outside vs. inside. See docs/ADDON_GUIDE.md for the new
probability-semantics section -- if your mod tuned `chance` under the
old per-roll model, you may want to bump those numbers.
