SaucedCarts v2.1.10
Released: 2026-05-30
====================

A one-fix hotfix for multiplayer: moving items between a held cart and a
bag lying on the ground now works.


BUG FIX
-------

* Multiplayer only: you couldn't move items INTO a held cart from a bag
  lying on the ground -- you'd start the transfer and nothing would
  happen. The reverse (moving items from a held cart OUT into a bag on
  the ground) put them in your main inventory instead of the bag.

  Both directions now work correctly. The item ends up where you told it
  to go.

  Single-player was never affected.


COMPATIBILITY
-------------

Save-safe. No save-data changes -- safe to upgrade mid-save.
